HOSTEL ITEM BORROWING SYSTEM

Students in university hostels or even hostels outside normally find a way to access or acquire certain items from their friends or neighbours incase they dont have them in possession.

Our task resolves around creating an app using OOP pricinples and lessons learnt from previous class to come up with a system that helps students and different hostels to borrow differnt items from each other.

The system built addresses some specific areas
Key Stakeholders & Components:
- Hostel & Warden: Manages registrations, room assignments, and resolves disputes.

- Students: Own, share, request, and return items.

- Items & Categories: Catalog available equipment by type and status.

- Agreement, Period & Conditions: Define terms, borrowing timeframes, and fine structures.

- Issues: Track broken rules, damaged goods, or late returns requiring escalation.
